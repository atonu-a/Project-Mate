About TeamNest

Built for builders of every kind

TeamNest is a place where people with an idea and people with the skills to build it can find each other — whether you write code, design interfaces, market products, write copy, or something else entirely. Great ideas shouldn't stay stuck as solo projects just because the right teammate was hard to find.

Why TeamNest

Most "find a co-founder" or "find a collaborator" platforms are built with developers in mind — everyone else gets left out. But building something real almost always takes more than one skill set. TeamNest exists so that a designer with no one to code for them, or a developer with no one to design for them, can post what they're working on and find people across every field who want to build it with them.

Who It's For

Builders & Developers

Find a designer, marketer, or co-founder for your next side project or startup idea.

Designers & Creatives

Partner with developers to bring your designs and prototypes to life.

Marketers & Writers

Join early-stage projects that need your voice before launch, not after.

Students & Beginners

Get real project experience by teaming up with people further along than you.

How It Works

1

Create your profile

Add your headline, field, skills, and a short bio so people know what you bring to a team.

2

Discover or post a project

Browse the feed for projects that need your skills, or publish your own idea and list the roles you're looking for.

3

Request to join, or review requests

Send a join request to a project you like, or review and accept requests from people who want to join yours.

4

Collaborate

Message your team, track progress, and build the thing you set out to build — together.

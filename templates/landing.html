Why TeamNest

Most "find a co-founder" or "find a collaborator" platforms are built with developers in mind — everyone else gets left out. But building something real almost always takes more than one skill set. TeamNest exists so that a designer with no one to code for them, or a developer with no one to design for them, can post what they're working on and find people across every field who want to build it with them.
